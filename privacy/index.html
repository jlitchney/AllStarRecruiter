Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

This policy explains how All-Star Talent (“we,” “us”) collects and uses information when you visit lawenforcementrecruiter.com (the “Site”). Law Enforcement Recruiter is a product of All-Star Talent. This policy covers this marketing website only. Use of the Law Enforcement Recruiter application at app.lawenforcementrecruiter.com is governed by the agreement with your agency.

Information we collect

Information you give us. When you schedule a demo, the booking form (provided by Calendly) asks for your name, email address, and, if you choose a phone call, a phone number, along with any notes you add. If you email or call us, we receive the information you share.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, the Site collects information about your visit, such as the pages you view, how you arrived (for example, from a search or an ad), your approximate location based on IP address, and your browser and device type. This is collected through cookies and similar technologies described below.

Campaign information. If you arrive through a link that includes campaign tags (such as utm_source or utm_campaign), the Site keeps those tags in your browser for the rest of your visit and passes them to Calendly with your booking, so we know which campaign led to it.

Cookies and tracking technologies

  • Google Analytics helps us understand how visitors use the Site. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
  • Google Ads conversion tracking tells us when someone who clicked one of our ads books a demo, so we can measure and improve our advertising. You can manage ad personalization in Google’s My Ad Center.
  • Calendly provides the embedded scheduling calendar and may set its own cookies, as described in Calendly’s privacy notice.

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Some parts of the Site, such as the scheduling calendar, may not work correctly without them. To learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

How we use information

  • To schedule and hold demos and respond to your questions
  • To follow up with you about Law Enforcement Recruiter and related All-Star Talent services
  • To understand how the Site is used and improve it
  • To measure the effectiveness of our advertising

We do not sell your personal information.

How information is shared

We share information only with service providers that help us run the Site and our business, including Google (analytics and advertising), Calendly (scheduling), and our website hosting provider, or when required by law.

Data retention

We keep demo and contact information for as long as needed to respond to you and maintain our business relationship, unless a longer period is required by law. Analytics data is retained according to our Google Analytics settings.

Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you, by emailing info@allstartalent.us. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable privacy laws, and we will honor requests as those laws require.

Children

The Site is intended for public agencies and professionals and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above shows when it was last changed.
